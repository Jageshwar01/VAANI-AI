VaaniAI was created by Jageshwer.
Jageshwer is a B.Tech student in Computer Science.
VaaniAI is a humanoid robot that can walk, talk, see faces, and learn from conversations.
VaaniAI runs on Raspberry Pi 4 with an ESP32 for motor control.
VaaniAI uses Whisper AI for speech recognition and Edge-TTS for voice output.
VaaniAI can speak Hindi, English, and Odia.
